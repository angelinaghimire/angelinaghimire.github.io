Automated Quality Assessment of Blind Sweep Obstetric Ultrasound for Improved Diagnosis

MIDL 2026 · in collaboration with GE HealthCare

Studied how real-world acquisition errors like reversed sweep direction, probe inversion, incomplete coverage degrade AI performance on obstetric ultrasound tasks (fetal presentation, placenta location, sweep classification), using a dataset of 8,000+ studies from clinical sites in the US and Zambia. Built automated quality-assessment models that detect these deviations with high accuracy, and showed that flagging and re-acquiring poor-quality sweeps meaningfully improves downstream diagnostic reliability.

Medical Imaging · Video Transformers (MViT) · Quality Assessment · Robustness

AI-Powered Task-Shifting for High-Quality Obstetrics Ultrasound Services in Community Healthcare Settings

Presentation · National Summit of Health and Population Scientists, Nepal

Co-authored a real-world implementation account of deploying an AI-assisted obstetric ultrasound system for task-shifting in low-resource community healthcare settings across Nepal covering system design, deployment logistics, and the practical challenges of introducing AI-supported care where devices, protocols, and provider training vary.

The Architecture of Attraction

How dating app interface design shapes user perception and trust

A comparative HCI study of Tinder and Bumble, examining how design choices like profile structure, swipe mechanics, messaging, color, and filtering shape user expectations and behavior. Combined a structural analysis of both platforms with a 54-person user survey and statistical triangulation.

Analyzing 51% Attacks in Blockchain

A review of consensus vulnerabilities and ML-based detection

A survey of 51% attack mechanics across Proof-of-Work and Proof-of-Stake systems, examining real-world incidents (Bitcoin SV, Ethereum Classic, Verge) and evaluating machine learning approaches — autoencoders, LSTMs, graph neural networks — for early attack detection. Proposes a multi-metric framework for evaluating blockchain resilience.
