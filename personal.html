Away from the keyboard

Outside work, I love...

books that make me underline half the page, films that leave me thinking about them days later, long walks with nowhere particular to go, and places that make me stop and look around for a while. I like little things too: a good cup of tea, beautiful stationery, conversations that accidentally last three hours, and finding a new song I want to play twenty times in a row.

This is the less serious corner of the website — a small collection of things I've loved, am loving, or keep coming back to.

Songs that feel slightly devastating at 1 a.m., plus whatever happens to be stuck in my head that week.
