Outside of Work...

other things I enjoy

  • reading until 2am and regretting it the next morning, every time
  • matcha lattes, oversized sweaters, and bookstores I never want to leave
  • baking elaborate desserts and eating most of the batter before they’re even in the oven
  • true crime documentaries, usually with the lights off and rarely a good idea before bed
  • getting carried away in conversations about philosophy, psychology, and why people are the way they are
  • yoga some mornings, skipping it on others, and feeling vaguely guilty either way
  • stargazing whenever I remember to look up and wonder what’s happening up there
