technology that keeps people at the center

Hi there. I'm fascinated by machines, minds, and ethics, and how we can use what we learn about them to build technology that actually helps people.

I studied Computer Science at Kathmandu University, where I explored a little bit of everything from algorithms, theory, and statistics to neural networks, human-computer interaction, and information systems ethics.

I became especially curious about intelligent systems: how they learn, how they reason, how they interact with us, and what it means to build things that are becoming increasingly intelligent. I'm still figuring out where these questions will take me, but I'd like to spend my career exploring them. Say hello →

Turns Out, Girls Can Do Tech

Women Leaders in Technology

I spent nine months as part of a 15-woman cohort, working through an intensive JavaScript, Node.js, and MongoDB bootcamp taught entirely by women, alongside leadership sessions on design thinking and problem-solving. I was also mentored one-on-one by Nora Asha Gurung.

Building to Win

Nepal AI School Hackathon

My team took first place in the Startup Track, designing and pitching a RAG-based grant document generator to a panel of researchers and industry experts.

Finding Community

Kathmandu University Computer Club

I served first as an Executive Member and later as Vice President of KUCC, Kathmandu University's departmental computer club. I co-led flagship events including KU IT Meet and KU Hackfest and organized workshops on emerging technologies.

I was also Editor-in-Chief of IT Express, where I led a 15-member editorial team through content strategy, editing, and design to publish 300+ copies reaching more than 1,500 readers.
