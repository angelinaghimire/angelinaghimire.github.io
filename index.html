Angelina Ghimire

technology that keeps people at the center

Hi there. I'm fascinated by machines, minds, and ethics, and by how what we learn about them can help us build technology that actually helps people. I studied Computer Science at Kathmandu University, where I explored a little bit of everything—from algorithms, theory, and statistics to neural networks, human-computer interaction, and information systems ethics.

I became especially curious about intelligent systems: how they learn, how they reason, how they interact with us, and what it means to build things that are becoming increasingly intelligent. I'm still figuring out where these questions will take me, but I'd like to spend my career exploring them—and hopefully make the world a little better along the way. If you have any ideas, reach out.
