Angelina Ghimire

technology that keeps people at the center

Hi there. I'm fascinated by machines, minds, and ethics, and how we can use what we learn about them to build technology that actually helps people. In undergrad, I studied CS and explored a little bit of everything—from algorithms, theory, and statistics to neural nets, human-computer interaction, and information system ethics. I became especially curious about intelligent systems: how they learn, reason, interact with us, and what it means for us to build things that are becoming increasingly intelligent. I’m still figuring out where these questions will take me, but I’d like to spend my career exploring them, and hopefully make the world a little better. If you have any ideas, reach out.
