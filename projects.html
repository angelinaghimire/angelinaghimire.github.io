Abhilekh

Image processing for Newari stone inscriptions

An image-processing system for preserving and studying historical Newari stone inscriptions. I implemented and compared thresholding methods including Sauvola, Otsu, Niblack, and FEAT, along with Fast Fourier Transform-based edge detection, to separate engraved text from the irregular surfaces and shadows of stone.

Python · Flutter · Flask · OpenCV · Image Processing
